# Roleplay Script: Safety Alert Conversation

**Scenario:** A technician says, "We've always done it this way."

**Response:**

I hear you. You know this site better than anyone, and the goal is not to make your shift harder.

The reason we are changing the walkway check is simple: the data shows a pattern we cannot ignore. Depot B has the highest number of slips during the night shift, especially between 10 PM and 2 AM. Most of those events did not cause a serious injury, but several had serious injury potential because people fell near moving equipment or hard surfaces.

So this is not about paperwork. It is about preventing the next person from going home hurt.

The change we are asking for is small: before the night shift gets busy, check the main walkway, clean spills immediately, and call out wet areas during handover. If a drain is blocked or lighting is poor, report it right away instead of working around it.

You have always found a way to get the job done. This update is about making sure everyone gets the job done and gets home safely.
